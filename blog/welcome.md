## A little about the work

I build software for businesses: web applications, APIs, internal tools, and the infrastructure that supports them. The work ranges from a focused website refresh to a larger application that connects customers, teams, and data.

This site is built with React, Material UI, and Vite. Journal posts are written in Markdown and turned into shareable, search-friendly pages during the production build.

## How I like to work

Good software starts with understanding the people who will use it. I like to get clear on the problem, agree on what success looks like, and shape a first step that is useful without making the project more complicated than it needs to be.

From there, I work in small, understandable steps and communicate trade-offs early. The goal is software that fits the way a business operates—not a pile of features that looks impressive but creates more work.

## A note on client work

Many client projects are private, so my public portfolio focuses on the kind of problems I’ve worked on and the outcomes the software supports. If you have a similar challenge, I’m happy to talk through the details of how I might approach it.

## What’s next?

I’ll use this journal for practical notes on software, product decisions, and the tools and ideas I’m exploring. If you have a project in mind, [get in touch](/contact) and tell me what you’re trying to make easier.
